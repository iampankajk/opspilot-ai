### Timing and double-counting rules

- **Active work** is time spent performing the logged activity. Record from participant observation or contemporaneous self-report, not inferred from message or event timestamps.
- **Waiting** is elapsed time awaiting a person, system, or response; record its reason and interval separately from active work.
- Do not count waiting as active work. Do not add overlapping waiting intervals together as if they were serial.
- Avoid double-counting active time. If activities overlap, split them into non-overlapping intervals where the participant can reasonably allocate effort; otherwise flag the overlap and leave the allocation unknown.
- The facilitator has configured no simulated specialist wait in this package. Facilitator response time during the tabletop is not fictional incident waiting time.
- Use only identifiers explicitly present in released synthetic records. Mark unavailable or unconstructed information as such; do not fill gaps with guesses.
